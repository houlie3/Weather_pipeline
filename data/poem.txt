Section 1:
Tomorrow's forecast is bright and fair,
Birthplace and Aalborg share the same air,
27 degrees Celsius, not a drop to spare,
While Previous Residence is slightly cooler to share.
The winds are gentle, a soothing breeze,
17.6 km/h in Birthplace, 18.0 km/h with ease,
In Previous Residence, a tiny bit more to seize.
