Section 1:
Tomorrow's forecast, a tale to tell,
Birthplace, Residence, and Aalborg to compare so well.
Temperatures similar, with highs of fourteen degrees,
Precipitation none, a pleasant breeze.
Wind speeds varied, but not by much, it's true,
A difference of one, in wind's gentle hue.
