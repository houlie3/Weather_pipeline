Section 1:
Tomorrow's forecast shows a similar scene,
Birthplace and Aalborg share the same sheen,
26.3 degrees with no rain in sight,
And winds that blow at 12.6 km/h light.
Previous Residence will be the hottest place,
28.5 degrees with a sunny face,
No precipitation, just a gentle breeze,
A perfect day, with warm and sunny tease.

Section 2:
I morgen vil vejrforholdene være lidt forskellige,
Men Birthplace, Aalborg og Previous Residence har alle sol,
Ingen regn, kun varme og let vind,
Men Previous Residence er varmest, det er bedst at finde,
Der vil det være allermest behageligt at være i morgen.
